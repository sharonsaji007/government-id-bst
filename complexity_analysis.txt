COMPLEXITY ANALYSIS

BST Search:
Best Case: O(1)
Average Case: O(log n)
Worst Case: O(n)

Linear Search:
Best Case: O(1)
Average Case: O(n)
Worst Case: O(n)

BST Insertion:
Best Case: O(1)
Average Case: O(log n)
Worst Case: O(n)

Inorder Traversal:
Best/Average/Worst Case: O(n)

BST Space Complexity:
O(n)

Inorder traversal auxiliary space:
O(h), where h is the height of the tree.

For string keys, the cost of comparing two keys can also
depend on the key length.
